Station Sciences launches survey for Duo Fold iPhone keyboard

A few quick questions to help us get Duo Fold to you faster.

Wow. I'm genuinely blown away by the support for Duo Fold (tentative name). More than 1,100 of you signed up in under a week, for a keyboard that exists as renders and a few early 3D printed prototypes.

We're working to get it into your hands as fast as we can. Here's a survey that will help us get it to you:

Take the survey →

If you're wondering why I'm building this, over the years I've owned almost every folding keyboard on the market, and all of them are lacking, most notably in the portability department. Most of them use normal staggered layouts, which split the keys in odd locations, ruining any chance you would have of memory typing and also managing to be really ugly.

The iPhone Duo is almost the perfect everything device for me. That said, one of the core problems with the Duo, in my opinion, is that the on-screen keyboard takes up to 50% of the screen. Like many people, I do plenty of dictation, but at the end of the day, there's no beating a real keyboard.

It may sound sappy, but a keyboard helps our dreams come true. Many of us feel that way when it comes to Apple products, and we'll do our best to make this keyboard feel the same.

One more thing: Duo Fold is still a working name. If you have a better one, the survey has a spot for suggestions, or just hit reply.
