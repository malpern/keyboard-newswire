Hey y'all,

Here are a few notable keyboard-related posts I thought were worth highlighting this time around:

  • There haven't been an issue or newsletter since the October’s switch overview and ranking update, so check out the community’s favorites if you haven't done so yet. Based on real vendor sales data as usual.

  • The IQUNIX EV63 EDG Edition is the most luxurious variant of the 60% EV63 gaming keyboard family - co-tuned by Edward Gaming's pros.

  • The PLAY1800, the first keyboard from TheKapCo, caters to a whole different audience. Paired with their cool Hot Wheels set, the typing feel and sound are surprisingly nice for a plastic case.

  • A low-pro unibody split keyboard with trackpad: Hachigo74 by baalsmn.
  • The one-handed RainDrop is a gaming keypad by AGorshkov101 -- with keywell and thumbstick.
  • Notaroomba's Keybytes is a flexible magnetic keyboard system built on 1u building blocks.
  • Sucralose by Codethetical updated: an even slimmer low-pro wireless split, now with a break-off outer column.
  • A wireless split ortholinear keyboard with various modules: Modula by taichan1113.
  • Matt Petiteau's Smallcat Uni 2 is a 26-key unibody split. Now even more compact.
