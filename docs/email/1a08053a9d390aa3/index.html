Xteink discontinues X4, replaces with X4 Classic

Three devices, one charging port now. Just not the one you'd want.

TL;DR: Xteink just made a choice I didn't expect.

Hi there 👋

Xteink discontinued the X4 last week and replaced it with the X4 Classic (X4 V2). I've spent some time rebuilding the guides, the comparison pages, and the specs on readme.club to match. Here's what actually changed, and why it left me a little unsettled.

What shipped

  • New device: the X4 Classic. Thinner (4.9mm vs 5.9mm), lighter (68g vs 77g), a motion sensor for one-handed page turns, and a customizable idle screen for when you're not reading.

  • New comparison guide: X4 Classic vs the original X4, what changed, what didn't, and whether you need to do anything if you already own one.

  • Every device guide and comparison page rewritten to match: /guide/x4, /guide/x4-vs-x4pro, /guide/x3-vs-x4.

  • If you already own the original X4, nothing changes for you. It still works exactly as it did.

The part that bugs me

The lineup is more consistent now. All three devices (X3, X4 Classic, X4 Pro) charge the same way: magnetic pogo-pin, with a USB-C adapter in the box.

That's tidier for Xteink. It's a regression for anyone who picked the X4 specifically because it was the one with a standard port.

Everyone else spent the last few years moving toward USB-C. Xteink just spent one moving away from it.

Apple's Lightning-to-USB-C switch got framed as a regulatory story. The EU forced their hand. It wasn't just that. It was also the thing a decade of iPhone owners had been asking for: one cable, not two. The regulation gave Apple cover to finally do the thing users actually wanted.

The X4 Classic does the opposite move, for no reason I can point to that isn't "product line consistency." You lose the one charging port everyone already owns a cable for, in exchange for a magnetic connector you now have to be careful not to lose the adapter for.

If I had to buy one today

Honestly, I'd hesitate. The thing that made the old X4 an easy recommendation (simple, no-adapter, borrow-anyone's-cable) isn't what's being sold under that name anymore.

But if I had to pick from what's actually on the shelf right now, I'd go X4 Pro. Not because of the charging (same pogo-pin as everyone else at this point), but because at $99 you get a frontlight, a touchscreen you can turn on or off, and (this is the detail that sold me) tap-to-look-up on a word for dictionary and translation. That's a genuinely useful feature for reading in a second language, and it's the kind of thing you only notice once you've used it.

$20 more than the Classic. Worth it, in my opinion.

That's the update. Guides are live, the old X4 pages redirect to the right place, and if you're trying to figure out which device to buy or whether yours got renamed under you, start here.
