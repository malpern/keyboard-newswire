Mode Designs opens pre-orders for Prologue keyboard

Our first 98% keyboard. Ships this December.

Prologue was designed as something you’ll use every day and see just as often.

Rounded corners, chamfered edges, sculpted rear surfaces, and clean visual seams help the larger form feel calm, balanced, and at home on the desk.

The typing experience was tuned the same way. Mounting system, plate design, force break pads, steel weights, feet, switches, and keycaps all shape the final feel and sound.

Keep an eye out for more sound tests and behind the scenes content soon.
