ZSA releases Wireless Voyager split keyboard

Have I got some news for you today: The Wireless Voyager is here. It's powered by QMK and fully supported in Oryx. It's got powerful rechargeable batteries that are user-replaceable with no soldering. It's the most ambitious split wireless keyboard on the market, and it is shipping now. This is not a pre-order or a crowdfunding hype drive.

You don't have to read any more. Just go check out the site, it has all the product details and images you could wish for. And if anything is missing, just reply to this email and ask us.

Launching a wireless keyboard is a huge change for us. We put in a lot of time and effort to arrive at a no-compromise product, one that delivers everything a wireless split ergonomic keyboard can be in a way that's never been done before. The full power of QMK on a wireless split board, those great batteries, and a trackball and a trackpad that snap on and deliver industry-leading performance.

This is the culmination of more than a year of intense hardware and firmware R&D, lab certification, relentless testing by the team and our external testers, and thousands of little details that had to come together just right for the Wireless Voyager to happen.

We did it. It's here, and it's amazing.

Over to you.

Erez
