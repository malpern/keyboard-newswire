ZSA launches Kailh Choc Platypus silent switch for Voyager

We just launched our very own low-profile switch, the Kailh Choc Platypus. It's purple, linear, and dead silent. It is the best silent Choc switch we've ever had the pleasure of using.

I thought my Voyager was already pretty quiet, then I installed the Platypus. Suddenly I was a stealthy typing ninja. My office turned eerily silent, yet characters kept appearing on the screen. I would send you a recording but I can't because it's so quiet. It's like the breath of a butterfly sleeping through a dreamless night.

The other thing is that the Platypus is very pleasant to type on. It's a 35gf switch, so it takes 35 grams of force to actuate. That's pretty light, but not to the point where you'll get accidental actuations. It's also very smooth — I almost called it buttery on the site, but I did not want to subject you to the word "buttery" (you have to read my emails for "buttery").
We like these so much that we made them the default switch for any new Voyager even though they cost us more so we make less for every board (we didn't increase the price of the Voyager).

We also have some sets for standalone sale. A set of 60 switches is $60 USD, plus $20 for shipping. Find these on the accessory store.

We've got less than 200 of these sets right now, so if you want one you should probably get it.
