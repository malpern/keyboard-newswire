Here are a few notable keyboard-related posts I thought were worth highlighting this time around:

  • Here are August’s top mechanical switches, the community’s favorites based on real sales data provided by 19 vendors and manufacturers.

  • Review: The IQUNIX EC75 is a Hall-effect gamer board. Pre-built, wired, with aluminum top case and intricate PC bottom design.

  • Stephan Møller's single-PCB tented unibody Leonardo KeycapRio underwent several revisions.
  • Hanthebot shared the Hall-effect variant of the Silakka54, a 54-key split keyboard.

  • Peaky 8-bit -- Toya Mimura's handheld, wireless, 2x4 key split chording keyboard for typing on the go: Peaky 8-bit.
  • Alex Miller introduces a new method to prevent ghosting of keyboards using the Japanese duplex matrix.
