KBD News Issue 217: Dygma Sonsei, XVX Trooper63 HE, and September switch sales

Keyboard Builders' Digest // Issue #217

Check out this week's editorial with some original content, awesome projects, tips, ideas, discount codes, etc!

Hey y'all,

Here are a few notable keyboard-related posts I thought were worth highlighting this time around:

  • Market overview: September’s top list of mechanical switches, the community’s favorites based on vendor sales data.

  • Review: A sleek prebuilt unibody split keyboard: Sonsei by Dygma -- for those looking for a high-quality in-stock option.

  • The Devil Breaker, a one-handed magnetic gaming keyboard by DarkBeacon, comes with TMR switches and various AI features.
  • Crazy. The Trooper63 HE by XVX features a kick-ass aluminum case, dual-diffuser HE switches, dual-RGB lighting, nice carbon plate -- for just $65 at the moment.
  • The Kemove K94 is a tri-mode wireless keyboard in the 96%/1800 form factor. Display, volume knob, VIA support. Great offer, now on sale.
  • Tiwaz is a split keyboard by Timo Strube. With gaskets and joysticks, each half works standalone as a one-handed controller.
  • A 60% wireless row-staggered split: the open-source Yuwol by Yuburoll.
  • The Translator is an ergonomic keyboard with mouse sensor -- by Feeflak.
  • A tiny split chording keyboard: Mesa 3 by David Brown.
  • TMRZ's Stagger keyboard is staggered in pretty much every possible sense.
  • This year's meme device by Gboard is the Conveyor Belt keyboard -- delivering keys straight to your fingertips.
  • A funky snap-on extension for your mechanical switches to make their actuation adjustable: TriggerKey by GMK.
